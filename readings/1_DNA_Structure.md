**DNA Structure and Heredity**  

DNA (Deoxyribonucleic Acid) is the hereditary molecule that defines the blueprint of life. Present in almost every living organism, it encodes the genetic instructions responsible for growth, development, and biological function.  

Acting as a molecular storage system, DNA carries the information required for protein synthesis, the process that powers nearly all cellular activities. Its unique double-helix structure allows accurate replication and ensures that genetic traits are reliably transmitted from one generation to the next.  

In bioinformatics, understanding DNA’s structure and information flow is fundamental. It’s the foundation for modern applications like genome sequencing, mutation analysis, personalized medicine, and disease prediction using machine learning.  



**Why This Matters**  

The study of DNA forms the backbone of computational biology. By integrating machine learning with genomic data, researchers can identify patterns in mutations, classify genetic disorders, and accelerate healthcare innovation through predictive analytics.  

**Insight** 

DNA doesn’t just store information, it defines the logic of life. Every step in computational genomics starts here, transforming raw biological data into insights that can save lives.
